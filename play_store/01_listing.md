Brain Battle Pro turns two spare minutes into a mental workout. Five fast brain games, a Daily Challenge, and score cards you can send to friends — who can play the exact same puzzle with your challenge code.

GAMES

• Logic Chain – spot the pattern and find the next number
• Math Blitz – true or false, at lightning speed

PROGRESS
• Daily Challenge with streaks and double XP
• Levels, rank titles and 18 achievements
• Brain Score radar chart showing your five skills
• Hall of Fame of your best scores on your device

CHALLENGE FRIENDS
• Share a beautiful score card image
• Friends type your code to play the identical puzzle — no account needed

PLAYS OFFLINE. No sign-up. Free with ads that never appear during a game.
