# Content rating, ads declaration, app access

## Ads declaration (App content → Ads)
- **Contains ads: Yes** (Google AdMob: adaptive banner, interstitial, rewarded)
- Ads appear only on menu/result screens and between games, never during play.

## Advertising ID declaration (App content → Advertising ID)
- **Uses Advertising ID: Yes** — purposes: *Advertising or marketing*, *Analytics*, *Fraud prevention, security and compliance* (Google Mobile Ads SDK declares the `AD_ID` permission automatically).

## Target audience and content
- Target age group: **13+** (do NOT select under-13: that would trigger the Families policy and its ad restrictions). Not designed for children; no child-directed appeal intended.
- News app: No. Government app: No. Health app: No. Financial features: No.

## Content rating (IARC questionnaire) — prepared answers
- Category: **Game**
- Violence / blood: No · Sexual content / nudity: No · Profanity: No · Controlled substances: No
- Gambling or simulated gambling: **No** (no betting, no loot boxes, no purchases)
- User-generated content / user interaction: **No** in-app (no chat or posting). Users can export a score image through the system share sheet.
- Shares user location: No · Digital purchases: No
- Expected result: lowest age tier (e.g. ESRB Everyone, PEGI 3). Final rating is issued by IARC — re-answer honestly if the app changes.

## App access for reviewers
No login, no paywall, no restricted areas. All features work immediately. Tip for reviewers: tap any game on the Play tab; the Daily Challenge is the large card on top. Ads: production ads will load only after the app is approved by AdMob; empty banners are normal on a brand-new ad unit.

## Other App content forms
- Data safety: see `03_privacy_and_data_safety.md`
- Government/financial/health: not applicable
- Account deletion: **not applicable** (the app has no accounts). If accounts are ever added, in-app deletion + a web deletion page are mandatory before release.
